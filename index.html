About

A shared table, not a single office

The Zamboanga–Basilan Integrated Development Alliance, Inc. is a consortium of local NGOs that pool their networks, field experience, and resources rather than working in isolation.

Member organizations reach communities that a single group could not reach alone — supporting local peacebuilding work, including observances like the annual Mindanao Week of Peace, alongside longer-running community development efforts.

"An alliance works because no single organization carries the whole weight."

Four member NGOs currently make up the alliance, spanning peacebuilding, community development, and grassroots organizing across the two provinces.

What we do

Focus areas

Peacebuilding

Dialogue and reconciliation work across communities, including the Mindanao Week of Peace.

Community development

Programs that strengthen local capacity, livelihoods, and self-reliance.

Youth leadership

Equipping young people to carry peace and development work in their own communities.

Partnership building

Connecting government, civil society, and donors around shared goals.
